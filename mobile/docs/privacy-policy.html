ANCHOR & OAK

Privacy Policy · Pralungo Labs · South Africa

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Pralungo Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes the Anchor & Oak mobile application (the “App”). This policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and applicable Google Play policies.

Summary
  • No user accounts in the current version of the App.
  • Your 30-day program progress is stored only on your device (local storage).
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • No in-app analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking in the current App.

1. Who we are

Responsible party (POPIA): Pralungo Labs
Country: South Africa
Contact: Pralungolabs@gmail.com

2. Information we process

2.1 Information stored on your device (not sent to our servers)

The App uses on-device storage (AsyncStorage) to save:

  • Which program days you have completed;
  • Your current day in the 30-day journey;
  • Whether you have seen certain in-app onboarding prompts.

This data stays on your phone or tablet unless you uninstall the App, clear app storage, or use a device backup feature offered by your operating system. We do not operate a cloud sync or user account service in this version of the App.

2.2 Purchases (Google Play)

If you buy the lifetime unlock, payment is handled by Google Play. Google may collect personal and financial information according to Google’s Privacy Policy. The App uses Google Play Billing (via expo-iap) to verify that you own the product lifetime_unlock and to unlock content on your device. We do not store your payment card number or bank details.

2.3 Information we do not collect in the App today

  • Name, email, or phone number (no sign-up);
  • Precise location;
  • Health records or clinical diagnoses;
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone/camera content;
  • Analytics or crash-reporting identifiers (no such SDKs are integrated in the current build).

3. Legal basis and purpose (POPIA)

We process limited information to:

  • Provide the App — remember your local progress (legitimate interest / performance of a service you requested);
  • Process purchases — verify entitlements through Google Play (contract / legal obligation where applicable).

We do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing and cross-border transfers

We do not share on-device progress data with third parties. Google Play processes payments under its own terms and may process data outside South Africa. When you use Google Play, you are also subject to Google’s policies.

5. Retention

  • On-device progress: retained until you delete the App or clear its storage.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google; we rely on Google Play to confirm ownership when you use Restore Purchases.

6. Security

Because progress data remains on your device, protect your device with a screen lock. Keep your Google account secure for purchase restoration.

7. Your rights (POPIA)

South African users may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold. Because we do not operate accounts or receive your on-device progress on our servers, most requests will relate to any correspondence you send us. Contact Pralungolabs@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

8. Children

The App addresses adult relationship and wellness topics. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child provided information to us, contact us at the email above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
