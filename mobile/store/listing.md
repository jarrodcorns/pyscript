## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Anchor & Oak is a calm, partner-focused program for people supporting a spouse or long-term partner through depression, anxiety, or medication-related changes—including emotional distance and intimacy stress.

This app is educational support for partners. It is not therapy, not a medical device, and not emergency care. It does not diagnose conditions or tell you to change medication. If someone is in danger, use local emergency services or the crisis lines in the Support tab (including South Africa helplines).

WHAT YOU GET
• A structured 30-day journey with daily reads, examples, and small consent-based practices
• Grounding tools, boundary language, and pressure-reduction ideas for home life
• A Learn library with evidence-informed articles on depression, anxiety, caregiver stress, and common medication side effects—written for partners, not clinicians
• Progress saved on your device; revisit any day at your own pace

ONE-TIME UNLOCK
The first day is available to explore. Unlock the full program with a single lifetime purchase (Google Play Billing). Restore Purchases is available if you reinstall or change phones (same Google account).

WHO IT’S FOR
• Partners who want practical language and rituals—not lectures or “fix them” scripts
• Couples navigating SSRI/SNRI side effects, emotional blunting, or low energy without turning intimacy into pressure
• People in South Africa and worldwide who prefer plain-English, non-clinical wording

WHAT IT IS NOT
• A substitute for psychiatrists, psychologists, or couple therapy
• A crisis counselling service
• A guarantee of relationship or health outcomes

Publisher: Pralungo Labs (South Africa). Privacy policy linked in the store listing.
```
